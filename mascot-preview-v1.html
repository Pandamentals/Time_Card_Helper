WHAT'S NEW

Squash & stretch
Body flattens on landing, stretches at the top of each bounce.
Follow-through
Arms, legs and bell are springs. They lag and overshoot the body.
Living face
Random blinks, eye saccades, brows and mouth move with the body.
Mood pop
Changing mood makes Tic flinch (worse) or jump (better).
Hover (desktop)
Tic leans in, looks at your cursor, raises brows and waves.
Click / tap
A quick hop with anticipation and landing squash.
Mood personality
Calm bobbing → fidgeting → continuous tremble → flailing panic. Over-target gets bouncier, then cheers.
Idle gags
On-target and over-target Tic occasionally hops or glances around.
